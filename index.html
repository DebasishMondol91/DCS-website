Who we are

Design Consultancy Services (DCS) is a services consultancy with a highly qualified, professional team in Electrical, HVAC, PHE and Fire Fighting services. The firm was established in 2012 under the guidance of Chinmoy Mandal.

We have developed an effective approach and methodology to execute projects successfully and complete and commission them on time, while keeping quality and cost under control.

Our resource base is equipped to handle light, power, networking and fire alarm system infrastructure of any kind.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

This privacy policy sets out how Design Consultancy Services (DCS) uses, stores, protects and manages any information you provide to us when you use this website.

What information do we collect about you?

We may collect the following information:

  • Information you provide when completing and submitting the enquiry form on this website. This information is stored securely in our database.
  • A record of correspondence if you contact us by email, telephone or WhatsApp.
  • Information you provide when you engage us for our consultancy services.
  • Website usage information collected using cookies.

How will we use the information about you?

We collect information about you to respond to your enquiry, prepare proposals and quotations, deliver and manage our services and, if you agree, to contact you about other services we think may be of interest to you. We may also use information collected from the website to improve your repeat visits.

We do not share your information for marketing purposes. We will not keep your data any longer than is necessary, unless it is required to comply with legal or regulatory requirements.

We are committed to ensuring that your information is stored securely. To prevent unauthorised access to your data, we have put suitable physical, electronic and managerial procedures in place.

Marketing

We would like to send you information about our services which may be of interest to you. If you have consented to receive marketing, you may opt out at any time by using the option at the bottom of the relevant email. If you no longer wish to be contacted for marketing purposes, please contact us.

Access to your information, correction and deletion

You have the right to request a copy of the information we hold about you, and to ask us to correct or delete it. To make a request, please email dcssystemphe@gmail.com.

Cookies

Cookies are text files or small pieces of data placed on your device to collect standard internet log and visitor behaviour information. This information is used to track visitor use of the website and to compile statistical reports on website activity.

A cookie in no way gives us access to your device or any information about you, outside of the data you choose to share with us. You can set your browser not to accept cookies.

Changes to our privacy policy
